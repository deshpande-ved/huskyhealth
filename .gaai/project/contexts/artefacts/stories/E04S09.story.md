# Story: ASCII husky mark and icons

## User Story

**As an** athlete,
**I want** the app to carry a husky mark drawn in the same ASCII style as the rest of the screen, with a matching home-screen icon,
**so that** the app looks like one piece of work and is easy to find on my phone.

---

## Context

Added by Ved's round-1 decision R-3 (DEC-18: no feature silently dropped). Design-ascii.md, Components: the mark is an ASCII husky made with `make_art.py` from an original photo (Ved's own, or CC0), never Northeastern's Paws logo (FE-5). Icons (192 and 512 PNGs, and the Apple touch icon) are rendered from the same mark, replacing the placeholder icons from E01S02.

This story is cuttable. If it is cut, E01S02's placeholder icons stay, and no other story depends on the mark (E04S07 walks whatever the app imports, so it stays valid).

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: manual (Ved): supplies `MARK`, the mark's text rows, converted from his own photo or a CC0 photo with `make_art.py` outside this repo. No photo is committed. The first comment line of `web/js/mark.js` names the source and its licence. STOP: if Ved has not supplied the MARK rows, stop and report; never invent them. Checked by Ved's review of the PR.
- [ ] AC2: `web/js/mark.js` exports `MARK` as 1 to 20 rows, each at most 44 columns, containing only characters from `RAMP` (E04S01) and spaces. Checked by `node --test tests/mark.test.js`.
- [ ] AC3: `web/index.html` has an empty `<pre role="img" aria-label="HuskyHealth husky mark">` in the header, and `web/js/app.js` fills it with `paint()` from `MARK`; no `innerHTML`. Checked by `node --test tests/mark.test.js` (reads `index.html`) and the FE-1 greps.
- [ ] AC4 (compliance, FE-5): `grep -rniE "paws|northeastern" web/ scripts/` returns nothing, and the source line in `mark.js` says "original" or "CC0". Checked by that grep and `node --test tests/mark.test.js`.
- [ ] AC5: `scripts/make-icons.mjs` (E01S02) draws `web/icons/icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` and `apple-touch-icon.png` from `MARK` on background `#191724`: each non-space character becomes a filled square whose brightness follows its `RAMP` index. The sizes stay 192, 512 and 180, and the corner pixel stays `#191724`. Checked by `node scripts/make-icons.mjs` then `node --test tests/mark.test.js` (IHDR sizes, corner pixel). manual (Ved): the home-screen icon on his iPhone shows the mark.
- [ ] AC6 (R-1): `web/sw.js` adds `/js/mark.js` to SHELL and bumps `CACHE`. Checked by `node --test tests/web-sw.test.js` and `grep -n "/js/mark.js" web/sw.js`.

> All acceptance criteria must be verifiable. Vague criteria are invalid.
