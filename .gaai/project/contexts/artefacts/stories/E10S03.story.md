# Story: Ask HuskyHealth from the app

## User Story

**As a** player,
**I want** a question box that shows me exactly what will be sent before I ask,
**so that** I can get answers about my training and trust what left my phone.

---

## Context

Feature F17 "Ask HuskyHealth", page half. **Cut together with E10S02**; the two ship or are cut as one unit, independent of F16, F18 and F19 (DEC-18).

- The page builds up to 12 weekly summaries from features on the phone (DEC-2): relative week index only, values rounded with the steps in `ai-brief-relay.md`. The shape is `WeekSummary` from E10S02.
- The user sees the exact JSON (question + summaries) and consents to Claude before anything is sent (PRIV-5).
- The answer and every lookup Claude made are shown. Nothing is stored.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1 (compliance): `weeklySummaries(features)` returns at most 12 items, each with only a week index 0–11 and rounded numbers / sport names; no key or value holds a date, time, device, source name or per-workout record. Checked by `node --test` with a synthetic features fixture (keys checked against `/date|time|start|end|device|source|name/`).
- [ ] AC2 (compliance): pressing Ask shows the exact JSON that will be sent and asks for Claude consent; without consent, or after Cancel, no request is made. Checked by `node --test` with a stubbed `fetch` that records calls.
- [ ] AC3: the answer is rendered with `textContent` and followed by a list of every lookup (`tool` + arguments); the question, summaries and answer are never written to IndexedDB, `localStorage` or `sessionStorage`. `web/js/weekly.js` and `web/js/ask.js` are added to `SHELL` in `web/sw.js` with a `CACHE` bump. Checked by `node --test` with storage stubs, the FE-1 greps in `frontend.rules.md`, and a grep of `web/sw.js`.
- [ ] AC4 (error-handling): offline, a 410, 422 or 502 shows a plain message ("Couldn't ask right now" / "That question was blocked: it looked like it had a name or place") and keeps the app usable; nothing is retried automatically. Checked by `node --test` with a stubbed `fetch` per status.
- [ ] AC5: manual (Ved): on the installed PWA, ask "How did my load this month compare to last month?"; the answer and its lookups show, and mitmweb's Request body tab for the Claude call shows the redacted question and rounded numbers only.
