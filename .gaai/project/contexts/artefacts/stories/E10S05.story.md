# Story: Record ARX risk and settle rounding widths

## User Story

**As** Ved, presenting the privacy story,
**I want** ARX's before-and-after risk numbers and rounding widths chosen from them,
**so that** the brief's de-identification claim rests on a measurement and a slide.

---

## Context

Feature F18 "ARX risk analysis", part 2. **Cut together with E10S04**; the two ship or are cut as one unit, independent of F16, F17 and F19 (DEC-18).

Plan steps 3–4: read the ARX risk view before and after rounding; copy chosen widths into `bucketize()`. Running ARX is a manual step by Ved (T-1). Delivery applies widths only after Ved records his choice, pastes the `risk.py` output and writes the slide text.

- **STOP:** start only when `docs/arx-results.md` holds Ved's recorded numbers and widths; otherwise stop.
- **STOP:** with ~20 columns all treated as quasi-identifying, the rounded cohort may still show k < 5 under any sensible widths. If no width set Ved accepts reaches k ≥ 5, don't keep widening: record the numbers as they are and report to Ved. The slide then says so plainly (the claim rests on no direct identifiers plus rounded aggregates).
- **STOP:** if ARX can't run on the Mac, stop and report; `risk.py` numbers alone are labelled "our check, not ARX".
- Changing rounding steps changes memory `ai-brief-relay.md`; Delivery reports it and Discovery updates memory.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1 (manual, Ved; checked at start by the STOP above): `docs/arx-results.md` holds Ved's recorded prosecutor, journalist and marketer risk before and after rounding, and his chosen widths, from running ARX with `docs/arx-steps.md` on both CSVs.
- [ ] AC2: if Ved's chosen widths equal the current rounding table, `bucketize()` is unchanged; otherwise `bucketize()` uses the new widths, the relay's rounding tests are updated to match, and `risk.py` on a regenerated `rounded.csv` is pasted into `docs/arx-results.md`. Checked by pytest and the pasted output.
- [ ] AC3 (compliance): rounding can only get coarser: a pytest asserts every width in `bucketize()` is ≥ its value in the current rounding table (`ai-brief-relay.md`), and `BriefIn` is unchanged (`git diff relay/schema.py` is empty).
- [ ] AC4 (compliance): `docs/arx-results.md` has a before/after slide text that cites ARX and NIST IR 8053, says "de-identified" and "re-identification risk", and never "anonymous" or "anonymized". Checked by the CLAIM-2 grep and the runbook D5 scrub grep.
- [ ] AC5 (error-handling): if the k ≥ 5 target isn't reached, `docs/arx-results.md` states the reached k and the honest wording from the STOP note, and no width is changed beyond Ved's choice. Checked by reading the file in review (manual (Ved) sign-off on the wording).
