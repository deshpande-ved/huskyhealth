# Story: Hold-to-talk effort check-in, OFF by default

## User Story

**As an** athlete after a squash session,
**I want** to hold a mic button and say my effort, only if I turned voice check-ins on,
**so that** I can rate effort hands-free, and the app never listens otherwise.

---

## Context

Typing a number is the default. A Settings toggle "Voice check-ins" (`voice_checkin`, in the E05S08 `db.js` settings store) is OFF on first run and after Forget (PRIV-4, DEC-17). Turning it on shows the Gradium consent for audio: what Gradium gets (≤ 5 s of audio per check-in) and that only a number comes back. When on, a hold-to-talk mic button appears next to the effort entry; the PWA records with `getUserMedia` while held (max 5 s), gets a listen token, posts to `/v1/listen`, and fills the effort entry with the returned number.

Split note: runbook story 4 is cut into E07S04 (relay) and this story (PWA). Cut together with E07S04.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1 (compliance): the `voice_checkin` setting in the `db.js` settings store is `false` by default and after Forget everything; with it off, no code path calls `getUserMedia` and no mic button is shown. Check: `node --test` asserts the default and that `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` (stubbed) is never called while off.
- [ ] AC2 (compliance): turning it on first shows a Gradium audio consent stating that up to 5 s of audio per check-in goes to Gradium and only a number 1–10 comes back; declining leaves it off, and the answer is stored as `gradium_listen_consent` (default false, cleared by Forget) in the `db.js` settings store. Check: `node --test`.
- [ ] AC3: holding the mic records at most 5 s (stops automatically), stops the mic track on release, gets a `kind=listen` token, posts the clip to `/v1/listen`, and puts the returned integer into the effort entry. The recorder's `mimeType` must be one of the audio formats E07S04 AC1 recorded in the `relay/gradium.py` header; if it is not, stop and report. Check: `node --test` with `MediaRecorder`, `getUserMedia` and `fetch` mocked asserts the 5 s cap, track stop, and the filled value; `grep -n "docs.gradium.ai" relay/gradium.py` shows the recorded formats, which the reviewer matches to `mimeType`.
- [ ] AC4 (error-handling): mic permission denied, 422 "no effort heard", 429, 410 or offline → a short plain message and the typed entry stays usable; nothing is retried with the same token. Check: `node --test` with mocked failures.
- [ ] AC5: FE-1 greps return nothing for the new code (`innerHTML|outerHTML|insertAdjacentHTML|document\.write` in `web/`; no `on*=` or `style="` in `web/**/*.html`); the button is a real `<button>` with an `aria-label`; `checkin.js` is in `SHELL` in `web/sw.js` with `CACHE` bumped (R-1). Check: those greps, `node --test`, and `node --test tests/web-sw.test.js`.
- [ ] AC6: manual (Ved): on the iPhone with check-ins on, hold the mic, say "seven", and 7 appears; mitmweb shows one Gradium request with audio, and the relay logs show no audio or transcript.

> All acceptance criteria must be verifiable. Vague criteria are invalid.
