# Story: Read the records and workouts we need from export.xml text

## User Story

**As an** athlete importing my Apple Health export,
**I want** the app to pick out only the heart, HRV, VO2 max, effort and workout data it needs from my 60 days,
**so that** it can score my training without reading anything else in the file.

---

## Context

`export.xml` can be gigabytes. The parser is a line scanner, not an XML DOM: every needed attribute sits on the opening-tag line of `<Record` / `<Workout`, and `<WorkoutStatistics` lines inside a workout carry active calories. Older exports put `totalEnergyBurned` on the Workout itself. This story builds that scanner (fed text lines, no file or zip handling yet) and the hand-written synthetic fixture the whole epic tests against.

Output is the internal `{ samples, workouts }` shape from the 6.3 sketch (`samples.hr|rhr|hrv|vo2|effort|effortEst` as `[ms, value]`; workouts with `type, start, end, min, kcal, source`). It never leaves the worker.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: `tests/fixtures/synthetic-export.xml` exists, hand-written and synthetic, and contains: self-closing Records; multi-line Records (a Record with child elements, e.g. `MetadataEntry` and an HRV `HeartRateVariabilityMetadataList` with `InstantaneousBeatsPerMinute` children, closed by `</Record>`); a Workout with `WorkoutStatistics` (ActiveEnergyBurned); an old-style Workout with `totalEnergyBurned` and no statistics; a self-closing Workout; a `WorkoutRoute` with a `FileReference` to `/workout-routes/…gpx`; an ECG entry; a `ClinicalRecord` entry; a record type outside the allowlist; a Record older than the 60-day window; every Workout is about 2 min long (duration = end − start) with at least 8 HR Records in its window (one per 15 s), except one sparse Workout with fewer than 8 (the `hr_sample_ok` false case, E03S03). Device names in it are fictional (no real person's name). Check: file review + `git check-ignore tests/fixtures/synthetic-export.xml` prints nothing (the fixture is committable, SAFE-1).
- [ ] AC2: Scanning the fixture yields exactly the expected kept samples per allowlisted type (HeartRate, RestingHeartRate, HeartRateVariabilitySDNN, VO2Max, WorkoutEffortScore, EstimatedWorkoutEffortScore) with `[ms, value]` pairs; `InstantaneousBeatsPerMinute` children are not counted as heart-rate samples. Check: `node --test tests/parse-apple.test.js`.
- [ ] AC3: Workouts come out with type, start, end, minutes, source and kcal: kcal from `WorkoutStatistics` ActiveEnergyBurned when present, else from `totalEnergyBurned`, else `null`; both self-closing and child-bearing Workouts are captured; items before local midnight of day −(window_days−1) (relative to an injectable `now`; not `now − 60×86 400 000 ms`) are dropped, and a named test shows a record on day −60 is dropped. Check: `node --test tests/parse-apple.test.js`.
- [ ] AC4 (compliance, DEC-13 / PRIV-3): the record-type allowlist is an explicit constant in `web/js/parse-apple.worker.js`; the fixture's route `FileReference`, ECG and clinical entries produce no output and their paths/values appear nowhere in the result. Check: a named test asserts this, plus `grep -nE "workout-routes|electrocardiograms|export_cda" web/js/parse-apple.worker.js` returns nothing.
- [ ] AC5 (compliance, PRIV-1): `grep -nE "fetch\(|XMLHttpRequest|WebSocket|EventSource|sendBeacon|importScripts|import\(['\"]https?:|from ['\"]https?:" web/js/parse-apple.worker.js` returns nothing; and `web/sw.js` SHELL lists the worker path with the cache version bumped (R-1; check: `grep -n "parse-apple.worker.js" web/sw.js`).
- [ ] AC6 (error-handling): a line with a missing or non-numeric `value`, an unparseable date, or a truncated tag is skipped without throwing, and scanning continues. Check: a named test in `tests/parse-apple.test.js` with such lines.

> All acceptance criteria must be verifiable. Vague criteria are invalid.
