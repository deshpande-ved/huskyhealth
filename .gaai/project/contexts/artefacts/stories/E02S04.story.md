# Story: Data gates

## User Story

**As an** athlete,
**I want** the app to tell me when my data is too thin to trust a number, and what to do about it,
**so that** I never act on a training call built on bad or missing data.

---

## Context

Fourth engine story: the 7.4 layer-4 data gates, plus one entry point that runs the whole engine on a features JSON so the gates sit next to the numbers they qualify.

Gates (7.4 layer 4):
1. workout HR averages ≥ 1 sample per 15 s, else that workout's zones are low-confidence;
2. fewer than 3 sessions reaching zone 4–5 → "max HR may be underestimated";
3. HRV on ≥ 3 of the last 7 days and 14 baseline days, else "not enough data" (from E02S03);
4. a workout with no effort → "rate your effort" (session-RPE needs it, DEC-8).

Gate 1 reads the per-workout `hr_sample_ok` boolean (≥ 1 HR sample per 15 s). E03 computes it in the features JSON (Ved decision R-4c). This story does not need E03: its tests use the synthetic fixture with that field set by hand.

---

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] AC1: `hrDensityGate(workouts)` lists each workout whose `hr_sample_ok` is `false` as `low_confidence` (its zones are flagged low-confidence); all `true` → no entry. Checked by `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"` on the fixture.
- [ ] AC2: Max HR gate: workouts where 2 sessions have minutes in zone 4 or 5 (`zone_min[3] + zone_min[4] > 0`) → `max_hr_low` gate raised; 3 sessions → not raised. Checked by `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"`.
- [ ] AC3: Effort gate: each workout with `effort` `null` is listed (by `day` and `sport`) under a `rate_effort` gate; none listed when all have effort. Checked by `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"`.
- [ ] AC4: `analyze(features)` on a synthetic fixture (`tests/fixtures/features-engine.json`, `v: 1`, 60 days) returns `{readiness, fitnessFatigue, monotony, loadSpike, vo2Trend, sportView:{<sport>}, gates:[{id, items, source}]}`, with `sportView` keyed by each sport present. The base 60-day fixture gives a `hard` or `easy` call; a variant that blanks HRV days so only 2 of the last 7 have HRV gives `not_enough_data` and raises the HRV gate. Checked by `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"`.
- [ ] AC5 (compliance, FE-2): a test walks the whole `analyze()` result and asserts every metric value and every gate has `source` with `method`, `status` and `version` (gates 1, 2 and 4 use new `SOURCES` entries with status `ours`). Checked by `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"`.
- [ ] AC6 (compliance, PRIV-1 / CLAIM-1): `analyze()` doesn't mutate its input; gate texts use training language only (`grep -niE "injury|diagnos|medical" web/js/engine.js` returns nothing except the one exempt line "training insight, not medical advice", CLAIM-1 / R-4e); `tests/engine/purity.test.mjs` passes; `CACHE` in `web/sw.js` is bumped, checked by `node --test tests/web-sw.test.js`; and the full scoped run `node --test "tests/**/*.test.{js,mjs}"` is green.

## Notes

- `hr_sample_ok` is defined by E03 (R-4c: at least 1 HR sample per 15 s). This story only reads it, from the fixture, so there is no E03 dependency.
- "Reaching zone 4–5" is read as any minutes in zone 4 or 5. If Discovery wants a minimum, that is a later change.
- `analyze()` reports monotony and load spike on daily TRIMP (the Banister input). Session-RPE totals stay available per day in the features; no second load series here.
- The fixture is hand-written synthetic data; it holds no real dates, names or device names (SAFE-1).
