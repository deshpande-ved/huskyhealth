# Epic: Relay and brief (F7, F9)

## Purpose

An athlete who has their training call on the phone can ask for a short coaching brief that explains it. Only about 20 rounded, de-identified numbers leave the phone, only after the athlete sees exactly what is sent and consents to the provider, and the brief can never change the call. Load alerts (monotony, load spike) ride along in the brief. Every privacy claim is provable: a strict allowlist schema, single-use tokens in RAM, a read-only relay with no volumes, no body logging, and an egress proxy that shows the numbers-only request to Claude.

---

## Scope

Source: plan 8.3, 10.2 (incl. the CORS map), 10.3, 10.5, with 8.1 privacy rules. Acceptance (binding, from the spec):

- `docker compose up` works on the Mac.
- The gateway exposes `/v1/` only, with a new `GET /v1/healthz`; CORS allows exactly the map's origins.
- `/v1/token` and `/v1/brief` with the strict `BriefIn` schema.
- Claude is called through egress; mitmweb shows a numbers-only body.
- Speak tokens are bound to the text's hash; all tokens are single-use (pytest).
- The relay container is read-only with no volumes.
- The PWA shows the exact `BriefIn` before sending (consent per provider, PRIV-5); the monotony and load-spike flags are included.

Also binding: PRIV-6 (no logging of bodies or `ValidationError` content, proven by pytest), PRIV-7 (tokens in RAM, one gunicorn worker), CLAIM-3 (the brief never changes the call), SAFE-4 (never `tailscale serve reset`), SAFE-5 (the Anthropic key lives only in the relay env, never on screen, never committed). Deploy to the Pi and Funnel (10.3) needs Ved's hands: those checks are "manual (Ved)".

## Out of Scope

- Presidio itself (E08). This epic leaves a named hook (`relay/pii.py: pii_types()`, a no-op stub) at the three points where E08 plugs Presidio in: incoming JSON strings, the outbound prompt, and Claude's brief.
- Presidio packages (E08) and the `cryptography` package (E06). E05 stays narrow: it adds neither, and `relay/requirements.txt` holds only `flask`, `gunicorn`, `pydantic>=2` and `anthropic`.
- `/v1/audit`, `replay.sh`, `pi-proof.sh` (E08 proof scripts).
- `/v1/speak`, `/v1/listen`, Gradium calls and voice UI (E07). E05 only mints the speak token bound to the brief text, and accepts `kind=listen` on `/v1/token`.
- Sync API routes (E06).
- Cloudflare Pages setup and `_headers` (E01).
- F16, F17, F19 AI features (E10).
