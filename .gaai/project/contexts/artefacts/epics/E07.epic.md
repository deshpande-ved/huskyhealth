# Epic: Gradium voice

## Purpose

An athlete can hear the readiness brief read aloud, in their own coach's voice, with each word highlighted as it is spoken. If they would rather nothing leaves the phone, the phone's own voice reads it, even in airplane mode. An athlete who turns voice check-ins on can say their effort ("seven") after a workout, and only that number survives.

This uses all three Gradium capabilities (speak, clone, listen), each with its own privacy rule (DEC-15), for the Best Use of Gradium prize. Pitch line: "We use voice to talk to you. We only listen when you ask, and only a number survives."

Features: F10 (spoken brief, captions, phone voice), F11 (coach's voice), F12 (voice effort check-in).

---

## Scope

- `/v1/speak` on the relay: sends the brief's exact text to Gradium through `egress:8082`, bound to the speak token, with an hourly limit. Paths, auth and body come from Gradium's official API reference, never guessed.
- In the PWA: a second, Gradium-only consent with a preview of the exact text; playback; word-highlight captions; the phone-voice fallback (on-device `speechSynthesis`).
- Coach's voice: a manual Gradium Studio clone by Ved of a consenting coach, then `GRADIUM_VOICE_ID`; the voice is labelled as an AI voice and deletable.
- `/v1/listen` on the relay: opt-in, OFF by default, ≤ 5 s audio, returns only an integer 1–10, hourly limit; the PWA's hold-to-talk check-in.

---

## Out of Scope

- Cloning any user's voice (never).
- Always-on listening, wake words, or any mic use while voice check-ins are off.
- Storing or logging audio, transcripts or brief text anywhere.
- The brief itself, `/v1/token`, `/v1/brief`, token minting and `SpeakIn` (E05).
- Presidio and the proof scripts (E08).
- Captions for the phone voice (the phone voice reads the text; captions follow Gradium playback only).
