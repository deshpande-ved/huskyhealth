HuskyHealth API

your Health data as a REST API · the server can't read it

todayimportaccountapi

What it is

Apple gives HealthKit no web API. HuskyHealth gives you one: your iPhone, your Watch's workouts, or any script can POST health records, and you can GET them back from anywhere. It is zero-knowledge. The server seals every record to your account's public key the moment it arrives and keeps only ciphertext. The private key lives inside your password-encrypted vault, so only you can open your records, in your browser or the CLI.

Who can read what

  • Device token: can add records. Cannot read anything.
  • The Pi: holds ciphertext, a hash of each token, a scrypt hash of your login key, your public key. Cannot read records, your username or your password.
  • You: log in with your password, which unlocks the private key on your device and opens your records there.

Endpoints

POST   /v1/me/records          device token   add a record (201 { id, at })
GET    /v1/me/records?after=N  session        your records, sealed
POST   /v1/account/tokens      session        new device token (shown once)
GET    /v1/account/tokens      session        list tokens (ids only)
DELETE /v1/account/tokens/{id} session        revoke a token
GET    /v1/openapi.json        none           full OpenAPI 3.1 spec

Base URL: https://veds-pi5.polecat-nominal.ts.net:10000. Records accept workouts (sport, start and end in seconds before sending, heart rate), resting HR, HRV and Watch effort. Names, places, GPS and device names are rejected.

Try it

1. Account page → New device token. 2. Send a record:

curl -X POST https://veds-pi5.polecat-nominal.ts.net:10000/v1/me/records \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HH_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"v":1,"workouts":[{"sport":"running","start_s":3600,"end_s":600,
       "hr":[{"t_s":3500,"v":142},{"t_s":3490,"v":145}]}],
       "effort":[{"t_s":600,"v":6}]}'

3. Read your records back, decrypted on your machine:

node scripts/huskyhealth.mjs records --user <you>

Or open the account page: logged in, it picks up new records within 30 seconds and adds them to today.
