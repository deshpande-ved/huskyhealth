Your Health data, over HTTP

Apple gives HealthKit no web API. This is yours: any device can POST your workouts, and you can GET them back from anywhere. The server stores only ciphertext and cannot read a single record.

01Send

The iPhone app posts each new Watch workout by itself. Scripts post with a device token, which can add records and never read them.

POST /v1/me/records
Authorization: Bearer hhw_…

02Sealed on arrival

The relay checks the record (no names, places, GPS or device names), encrypts it to your account's public key, and keeps only this:

{ "id": 7, "at": 1794103242,
  "key": "Qm9…", "nonce": "x2…",
  "ct": "8fJ…" }

03Read with your key

Your private key lives inside your password-encrypted vault. Log in on the website or the CLI and your records open on your device, nowhere else.

GET /v1/me/records?after=6
→ decrypted on your device

Base URL https://veds-pi5.polecat-nominal.ts.net:10000. A record holds workouts (sport, start and end as seconds before sending, heart rate), resting heart rate, HRV and Watch effort.

06Try it

Make a token on ./account, then send a run:

curl -X POST https://veds-pi5.polecat-nominal.ts.net:10000/v1/me/records \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $HH_TOKEN" -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"v":1,"workouts":[{"sport":"running","start_s":3600,"end_s":600,
       "hr":[{"t_s":3500,"v":142},{"t_s":3490,"v":145}]}],
       "effort":[{"t_s":600,"v":6}]}'

Read it back, decrypted on your machine:

node scripts/huskyhealth.mjs records --user <you>

Or keep ./account open while logged in: new records arrive within 30 seconds and fold into today.
